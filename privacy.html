Lukeolafp Claude Integration

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 · Last updated 20 September 2026

This policy covers the Lukeolafp Claude Integration (“the app”), a private tool built and operated by Luke (@lukeolafp) in Canada. The app connects the developer’s own social media accounts — TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Threads and Facebook — to software running on the developer’s personal computer, so that posts and statistics can be reviewed and drafted in one place.

The app has one user: its developer. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, collects nothing from visitors to this page, and never accesses anyone else’s account.

Information the app accesses

With the account owner’s permission, and only for accounts the developer owns, the app may read:

  • Basic profile information, such as username, display name, profile picture, and follower and video counts.
  • Content information, such as the titles, descriptions, links and posting dates of videos and posts on those accounts.
  • Performance statistics, such as views, likes, comments and shares.
  • Comments and replies left publicly on that content.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform, which allow the app to make these requests.

The app does not access private messages, contact lists, payment details, or the accounts of other people.

How the information is used

Solely to let the developer view and organise his own accounts: checking how posts performed, keeping records in a private notebook, and drafting new posts. The information is never used for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision affecting other people.

Where the information is stored

Access tokens and any saved data are kept in files on the developer’s personal computer, which is password-protected. The app has no server, no database, and no hosted account system. Nothing is stored on this website.

Sharing

The information is not sold, rented, or traded, and is not shared for advertising. It is disclosed only in two situations:

  • To Anthropic. The app works through Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Content the developer chooses to review is sent to Anthropic for processing, under Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • If the law requires it. Where a valid legal request compels disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is useful to the developer, and can be erased at any time by deleting the local files. Access to any connected account can be withdrawn from that platform’s own settings, which immediately stops the app from reading anything further.

Security

Access tokens are stored outside of any synced or shared folder and are never published, logged in shared documents, or committed to code repositories. No system is perfectly secure, so no absolute guarantee is given.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its developer.

Platform terms

Use of each platform’s data also falls under that platform’s own developer terms and privacy policies, including those of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page is updated and the “last updated” date above is changed.
